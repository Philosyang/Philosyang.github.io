## Driving
Ever since I got my first car (portable home) am I passionate in driving. Ironically, I was thinking of never needing a driving license back when I was in my university campustown. Driving solo means a controlled environment where I can listen to my favorite music while also giving me a sense of control and a feeling of progression. Driving with friends next to you means cozy talks.

My first car is a used decade-plus-old mini cooper that didn't really receive proper maintenance by the previous owner; I bought it mainly because of an extremely low price and an increasing need for driving (I don't want to take part in a running/biking session every time I want a burger.) It is definitely a car with its personalities. Despite everything, I had fantastic times with it; it was my favorite times each week to drive an hour north through the peninsula, SF, then over the golden gate bridge for some maimai during the weekends. The insurance is also extremely cheap since it needs not a collision insurnace.  
This car encountered a head gasket failure and a water pump leak in less than a year of my ownership, not to mention the continuously worse than preferred oil burn that really needs me to check and top off after each weekend drive. I ultimately decided to part ways after I saved enough money for a more reliable one, which leads to

A toyota corolla hybrid. After all the memories with the cooper, I wanted my next car to be reliable and low-maintenance. I opted for new cars only given my less than ideal experience with used cars. After quite some research (as well as regional considerations e.g. electricity / gas prices), I decided on a corolla hybrid. The electrification in harmony with the eCVT transmission as well as the 60+ mpg numbers really delights me every time I drive. I plan to drive this car for a long time unless there is a new total-cost-of-ownership winner by a wide margin.
